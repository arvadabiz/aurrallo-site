Aurrallo Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account (name, email address) and information necessary to connect your QuickBooks Online account via OAuth. We store access tokens in encrypted form — we never store your QuickBooks password. We also sync invoice data from QuickBooks solely to operate the reminder service on your behalf.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to: authenticate you, sync invoices from QuickBooks Online, send payment reminder emails to your clients on your behalf, and provide billing services through Stripe. We do not sell your data or your clients' data to any third party.

3. Data Sharing

We share data only with the following sub-processors required to operate the service:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Intuit (QuickBooks) — invoice data via OAuth
  • Stripe — payment processing for subscriptions
  • Resend — transactional email delivery
  • Railway — API hosting infrastructure
  • Cloudflare — web hosting and CDN

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for the duration of your account. You may delete your account at any time from the Settings page, which removes your personal data and disconnects your QuickBooks integration. Stripe retains billing records as required by financial regulations.

5. Security

OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256-GCM. All API communication uses HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

6. Your Rights

You may access, correct, or delete your personal data by visiting Settings or contacting us. California residents have additional rights under CCPA.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@aurrallo.com.
